# Typer App: Quick Start Guide

Welcome to Typer! This app helps students learn to type and communicate using a highly customizable, accessible interface. This guide walks you through setting up the app as a teacher/parent and how the student interacts with it.

---

## 1. Getting Started: Profiles
When you first open the app, you will see the **Profile Selection Screen**.
- **Teacher Profile:** The app automatically comes with a permanent **Teacher** profile (identified by the teacher avatar and light green background). This profile cannot be deleted. It is where you can build curriculum that can be shared across all students on this device.
- **Student Profiles:** Every student has their own unique data — themes, phrases, and history. Each student's app looks and behaves for them alone.
- **Create a Profile:** Click **Create Profile** and type the student's name, then pick an avatar (Fox, Elephant, Pig, or Octopus).
- **Select a Profile:** Click a student's card (each has its own pastel color) to enter their personalized Main Menu.
- **Delete a Profile:** Click the red **delete icon** on the student's card. Typer asks a grown-ups-only question first, and always saves a backup file to your Documents/Typer folder before deleting. (You can also drag a card onto the trash button in the corner — the delete icon is the easy way.)

---

## 2. The Main Menu
Once inside a profile:
- **Free Typing:** An open sandbox where the student communicates freely and independently.
- **Module 1: Words:** The core learning area for practicing single words.
- **Module 2: Phrases:** Practice typing full sentences from the student's active phrases.
- **Phrasebook:** A quick shortcut that reads the student's saved phrases aloud.
- **Words & Phrases:** Opens the teacher dashboard for building the curriculum (grown-ups only).
- **Switch Account (Top Right):** Return to the Profile Selection Screen.
- **Settings Gear (Top Right):** Voice, fonts, export, and updates (grown-ups only).
- **User Manual (? icon):** Top left of every screen — this guide!

---

## 3. Teacher Settings
Click the gear icon in the Main Menu (a grown-ups-only question opens first).

> [!TIP]
> **Voice Profile:** Choose BOY, GIRL, MAN, or WOMAN. Use **Test Voice** to hear it.
> **Use Google Cloud Voices:** Toggle ON and paste your own Google API Key for natural-sounding Google voices. The key stays in memory only (never saved) and is forgotten when the app closes. With the toggle OFF (or without a key), the app uses the device's built-in voice — always free.
> **Reading Font:** Pick the typeface used across the whole app — Fredoka (rounded and friendly), Lexend (easy to read), or Andika (for new readers).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Moving a profile to another device:** Open Settings, scroll to **Export Profile**, then click **Copy Share Code** (sends everything — themes, phrases, history — as text you can email or chat) or **Save Backup File** (writes a file to Documents/Typer). On the other device, open the Profile Selection screen, click **Import Profile**, and either choose the backup file or expand **Advanced: paste a share code instead**. If the profile already exists there, Typer offers to Safely Merge or Overwrite — and backs up first either way.

## 4. Words & Phrases (The Teacher Dashboard)
From the Main Menu, click **Words & Phrases**. It has three tabs:

### Word Theme Scheduler
Decides what the student practices each day.
- **Available Themes (Left):** Every theme created on this profile. Click a card's **edit icon** to jump straight to its words, or double-tap it.
- **Schedule (Right):** A 7-day calendar.
- **How to use:** Drag a theme from the left onto a day ("Today", "Tomorrow", ...). When the student opens Module 1 that day, they practice exactly those themes. Remove a scheduled theme with the ✕ on its chip (Typer confirms first).

### Word Setup
Where lessons are built.
- Select a theme from the dropdown, create a new one, **rename** it (pencil icon), or **delete** it (trash icon — confirms first).
- Add words to the selected theme one at a time; each word shows how many times it was spoken.

### Phrasebook Tab
Manages full sentences and communication.
- **Free Typing Log (Left):** Sentences the student typed in Free Typing. Review them and click **+** to save one permanently. Clear Log asks first.
- **Saved Phrasebook (Right):** Approved phrases.
  - Checkbox: turn a phrase active/inactive (active phrases appear in Module 2).
  - Star: pin to the student's Top 10 (maximum 10).
  - Pencil (or double-click): edit the wording. Trash: delete (confirms first).
- **Other Profiles:** Click this button in any tab to import themes and phrases from other profiles on this device — individually or all at once. You can also clear every profile's words and phrases from here (backed up first).

> [!NOTE]
> Keep phrases to letters, numbers, spaces, and these marks: ' - : ; , . ? ! — those are the keys on the student's keyboard. Typer warns you if a saved phrase contains something the keyboard cannot type.

## 5. Module 1: Words (Student View)
When the student clicks **Module 1**, they enter their learning environment.
- The app checks the **Word Theme Scheduler** for today's themes. If none are scheduled, it says so and practices starter words instead.
- Teachers can use the top-right dropdown to practice "Every theme on this profile" or a single theme.
- **Word List Panel:** The list icon in the app bar opens a side panel listing every practice word, with live green/red letter feedback. Toggle **Hide Word at Bottom** for an auditory spelling quiz!
- A distraction-free keyboard sits at the bottom; the **123+** key reveals numbers and symbols.
- Wrong letters shake and show a gentle hint instead of being accepted — press the correct letter to continue.
- Completing the word turns it green and unlocks SPEAK, REPEAT, and NEXT buttons.

---

## 6. Module 2: Phrases (Student View)
Module 2 moves from single words to full sentences.
- Phrases come from the student's **active Phrasebook** entries.
- The student types the full phrase, including spaces (the spacebar speaks the finished word).
- Press `ENTER` once to hear the phrase; press again to load the next one.
- If no phrases are active yet, the screen says so instead of leaving a mystery phrase.
- Every completed phrase increases its Play Count, showing which phrases are practiced most.

---

## 7. Free Typing (Communication View)
An open sandbox where the student communicates freely.
- **Typing:** Anything they type appears in massive, easy-to-read letters. Unfinished typing is saved as a draft automatically and comes back when they return.
- **Speaking Words:** Each `SPACE` speaks the word just finished, letter by letter.
- **Speaking Sentences:** `ENTER` speaks the entire sentence and files it into the history shown in green.
- **The Giant SPEAK Button:** Vocalizes whatever is currently typed.
- **History:** Recently spoken phrases stay visible so the student can see their conversation.
- Leaving with unfinished typing asks first: keep typing, or save and leave. Either way, nothing is lost.

> [!NOTE]
> Everything submitted with `ENTER` in Free Typing is logged to **Words & Phrases → Phrasebook tab → Free Typing Log** so you can review their progress later.
