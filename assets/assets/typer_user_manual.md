# Typer App: Quick Start Guide 🚀

Welcome to Typer! This app is designed to help students learn to type and communicate using a highly customizable, accessible interface. This guide will walk you through setting up the app as a teacher/parent and how the student interacts with it.

---

## 1. Getting Started: Profiles 👤
When you first open the app, you will see the **Profile Selection Screen**.
- **Teacher Profile:** The app automatically comes with a permanent **Teacher** profile (easily identified by the Teacher Owl avatar and light green background). This profile cannot be deleted. The teacher profile is where you can build curriculum that can be shared across all students.
- **Student Profiles:** Every student has their own unique data. John's app will look and behave differently than James's.
- **Create a Profile:** Click "Add New Profile" and type the student's name. You can select a fun avatar (Fox, Elephant, Pig, Octopus) for them!
- **Select a Profile:** Click on a student's card (which has a unique random pastel color) to enter their personalized Main Menu.
- **Delete a Profile:** Need to remove a student? Simply **Click and Drag** their profile card into the Trash Bin floating in the bottom right corner of the screen! *(Note: The Teacher profile cannot be deleted).*

---

## 2. The Main Menu 🏠
Once inside a profile, you have a few options:
- **Free Typing:** An open sandbox for the student to communicate freely and independently.
- **Module 1: Words:** The core learning area where the student practices typing single words.
- **Module 2: Phrases:** The advanced area where the student practices typing full sentences pulled from their Phrasebook.
- **Phrasebook:** A quick shortcut to manage the student's phrases directly from the main menu.
- **Switch Account (Top Right):** Go back to the Profile Selection Screen.
- **Settings / Gearbox (Top Right):** Access teacher tools, voice settings, and the Word & Phrase Setup.
- **User Manual (? Icon):** Located on the top left of every screen, this button instantly brings up this manual!

---

## 3. Teacher Settings ⚙️
Click the **Gearbox** icon in the Main Menu to access settings.

> [!TIP]
> **Voice Profile:** Here you can select the student's voice preference (BOY, GIRL, MAN, WOMAN).
> **Ultra-Realistic AI Voices:** Toggle this ON and securely paste your own Google Cloud API Key to upgrade the app to use lifelike neural voices. If left blank, the app will fall back to offline, system-generated voices with customized pitch adjustments.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Exporting Profiles:** At the bottom, you can click **Copy Profile Code**. This copies all of the student's data (themes, phrases, history). You can email this code to another tablet or therapist, and they can click "Import Profile" to instantly copy the student's entire setup!

---

## 4. Word & Phrase Setup (The Teacher Dashboard) 📝
Inside the Settings screen, click **Word & Phrase Setup**. This is where you build the curriculum. It has three main tabs:

### 📅 Word Theme Scheduler
This dictates what the student will learn on any given day.
- **Available Themes (Left):** A list of all created themes.
- **Schedule (Right):** A 7-day calendar. 
- **How to use:** Simply **drag** a theme from the left side and **drop** it onto a specific day (like "Today" or "Tomorrow"). When the student opens Module 1 on that day, they will only be quizzed on those scheduled themes!

### ✏️ Word Setup
This is where you build the actual lessons.
- Create a **New Theme** (e.g., "ANIMALS").
- Click into the theme to add specific words (e.g., "DOG", "CAT").
- You can attach custom images to each word to help the student associate the image with the spelling.

### 💬 Phrasebook & The Global Bank
This tab manages full sentences and communication.
- **Free Typing Log (Left):** Every time the student uses the Free Typing module and presses `ENTER`, the sentence they typed is recorded here. You can review what they wanted to say and click the **[+]** button to permanently save it.
- **Saved Phrasebook (Right):** A list of approved phrases. 
  - Check/Uncheck the box to enable or disable a phrase.
  - Click the **Star** icon to pin it to their "Top 10" easily accessible phrases.
- **The Global Bank:** Are you logged into the Teacher Profile? Any themes or phrases you create here act as the master "Global Bank". When you switch to a Student Profile, you can click the **Global Bank** button at the top to import those pre-made themes and phrases directly into the student's account!

---

## 5. Module 1: Words (Student View) 🎓
When the student clicks **Module 1** from the Main Menu, they enter their learning environment.
- The app will look at the **Word Theme Scheduler** to see what themes are scheduled for today.
- **ALL THEMES Override:** Teachers can use the top right dropdown to select "ALL THEMES" to completely shuffle words from every single theme in the app.
- **Word List Panel & Spelling Quizzes:** Click the List icon in the top right to open a persistent side-panel showing all active words. The teacher can toggle "Hide Word at Bottom" to turn the screen blank, turning it into an auditory spelling quiz while tracking progress in real-time on the side panel!
- A custom, distraction-free keyboard sits at the bottom.
- When they successfully type the word, the app speaks the word out loud (if TTS is enabled) and moves to the next image.

---

## 6. Module 2: Phrases (Student View) 🗣️
When the student has mastered individual words, they can move to **Module 2**, which focuses on typing full sentences.
- The app loads active sentences directly from the **Saved Phrasebook**.
- The student must type the full phrase, including spaces.
- When they finish typing, pressing `ENTER` will speak the full phrase out loud.
- Pressing `ENTER` again loads the next phrase.
- Every phrase they complete here increases its "Play Count" in the Phrasebook, helping you track which phrases they practice the most!

---

## 7. Free Typing (Communication View) ⌨️
This is an open sandbox where the student can communicate freely.
- **Typing:** The student uses the keyboard to type anything they want. The text is massive and easy to read.
- **Speaking Words:** Every time they hit the `SPACE` bar, the app will speak the word they just finished typing.
- **Speaking Sentences:** Hitting `ENTER` will speak the entire sentence and log it to the screen. 
- **The Giant SPEAK Button:** Located right above the keyboard, the student can press this at any time to have the app vocalize whatever they are currently typing.
- **History:** The green text in the middle of the screen shows a history of their recently spoken phrases so they can see their conversation!

> [!NOTE]
> Everything typed and submitted via `ENTER` in the Free Typing screen is securely logged and sent to the **Phrasebook -> Free Typing Log** in the Teacher Setup so you can review their progress later!
